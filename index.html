Inside Karimnagar's Culinary Revolution

Over the past few years, Karimnagar has transformed into a thriving epicenter of modern culinary culture in Northern Telangana. Beyond the traditional tea houses that built the town's social fabric, a brand new wave of passionate restaurateurs and baristas has introduced global gastronomical trends.

From 24-hour dessert sanctuaries serving viral Dubai Pistachio Kunafa bars on Gandhi Road, to third-wave manual V60 pour-overs in Christian Colony, authentic Taiwanese Boba tea near Jyothismathi, and weekend Halal Ramen in Vavilalapally, Karimnagar's cafe scene is as diverse as it is ambitious.
