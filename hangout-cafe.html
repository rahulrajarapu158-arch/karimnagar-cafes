Heritage & Atmosphere

A Welcoming Sanctuary in Ashoknagar

Perched next to ABR Cinemas near Kaman Circle, Hangout Cafe holds a rare perfect 5.0-star rating among Karimnagar's cafe community. Known as the neighborhood's ultimate post-movie hangout, we serve signature steaming Karak Chai, authentic rumali chicken shawarmas, and mountain-high loaded cheesy fries throughout the night.

Every beverage is extracted with passion using artisan roasted beans. Whether you arrive for a quiet morning espresso, an afternoon remote work session, or an evening celebration with friends, our team welcomes you with warmth.
