Heritage & Atmosphere

A Welcoming Sanctuary in Jyothinagar

Situated on the first floor above La'Delicious on Shiva Theater Road, Charlie Cafe & Lounge is Karimnagar's premier family and celebration destination. Designed with warm architectural lighting, plush velvet booth seating, and modern acoustic flair, our menu bridges vibrant continental appetizers, regional Telangana specialties, rich Hyderabadi biryanis, and handcrafted European desserts.

Every beverage is extracted with passion using artisan roasted beans. Whether you arrive for a quiet morning espresso, an afternoon remote work session, or an evening celebration with friends, our team welcomes you with warmth.
