Heritage & Atmosphere

A Welcoming Sanctuary in Vidyanagar

Bringing cosmopolitan London cafe sophistication to Vidyanagar on Malkapur Road, London Cafe is renowned for handcrafting all pizza doughs, brioche buns, and gourmet sauces entirely in-house. Boasting an impressive 4.9-star rating, we curate decadent crispy chicken burgers, loaded peri-peri fries, artisan shakes, and British-inspired combos.

Every beverage is extracted with passion using artisan roasted beans. Whether you arrive for a quiet morning espresso, an afternoon remote work session, or an evening celebration with friends, our team welcomes you with warmth.
