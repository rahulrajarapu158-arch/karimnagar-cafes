Our Heritage & Atmosphere

A Welcoming Sanctuary in Mukarampura

Conveniently situated right beside the iconic Mamatha Theatre on Collector Office Road, Hungry Birds is Mukarampura's favorite high-energy cafe. Opening early at 6:30 AM and running till 11:30 PM, we serve moviegoers, morning commuters, and nighttime snackers with golden crispy fried chicken, sizzling wraps, and comforting hot chai.

Every beverage is meticulously extracted using high-grade beans and ingredients. Whether you come for quiet afternoon contemplation, an evening family feast, or late-night takeout, our warm staff is ready to greet you with genuine hospitality.
